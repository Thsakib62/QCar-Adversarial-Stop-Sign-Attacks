# QCar Demonstration Video

`ACCESS_DEMO.mp4` is a 69.6-second, 1280 x 720, 30 FPS edited demonstration supplied with the accepted-paper materials. It combines the front-camera view, a LiDAR scatter plot, terminal output, and an external view of the testbed.

The opening segment is labeled a clean baseline run. Around 33 seconds, a title introduces a Lu3 attacked-sign run; the video then displays a collision caption. Around 45 seconds, a title introduces a Lu3 mitigation run. The final segment shows detections and the QCar continuing through the course. The titles identify the scenarios as provided by the authors; this edited video is not a timestamped raw run log, and no specific statistical run ID or checkpoint hash has been independently linked to its segments.

Use the video as a qualitative illustration only. Quantitative missed-stop outcomes, sample sizes, and statistical tests are based on the preserved run-level tables under `data/`, not on this clip. The clip should not be cited as proof of detector-only causality, generalization to untested attacks, or preservation of clean-sign performance after mitigation.
