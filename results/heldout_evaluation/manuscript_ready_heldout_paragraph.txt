The auxiliary held-out test evaluation was performed on a range-separated collection comprising continuous frames 0306-0346. Under the dataset naming convention, all 54 stop-sign annotations in this split are attacked signs, but individual attack designs are not encoded per frame. The COCO-pretrained YOLOv5n auxiliary checkpoint, fine-tuned with backbone layers 0-9 frozen and the detection head updated, achieved 86.15% mAP@0.5 and 51.77% mAP@0.5:0.95 on the 41-image held-out set. For attacked stop signs, precision was 87.19%, recall was 94.44%, and AP@0.5 was 94.55%. This checkpoint differs from the model associated with the physical mitigation runs. Because explicit recording-session and per-frame attack-design identifiers were unavailable, the split does not establish generalization to unseen attack types or independent acquisition sessions. These results characterize auxiliary detector-level performance, while the primary mitigation evidence remains the closed-loop QCar evaluation.
